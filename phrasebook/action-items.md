# Action-Item Phrases

## Summarize

- `Let me summarize the action items.` — 我来总结一下行动项。
- `We have two next steps.` — 我们有两个后续步骤。
- `To summarize, we will test the change first.` — 总结一下，我们会先测试这个变更。

## Confirm Ownership

- `I will take care of the test.` — 我会负责测试。
- `Could you confirm who will own this task?` — 可以确认一下谁负责这项任务吗？
- `The project team will provide the update.` — 项目团队会提供更新。
- `I can take ownership of this task.` — 我可以负责这项任务。
- `Who will take the next step?` — 谁来负责下一步？

## Confirm Time

- `The target date is Friday, correct?` — 目标日期是周五，对吗？
- `We expect to finish it by the end of the day.` — 我们预计今天结束前完成。
- `Please let us know if the schedule changes.` — 如果时间安排有变化，请通知我们。
- `Can we agree on a target date?` — 我们可以确定一个目标日期吗？
- `Let us review the progress on Friday.` — 我们周五再检查一下进展。

## Close the Meeting

- `I will send an update after the meeting.` — 我会在会后发送更新。
- `Please let me know if I missed anything.` — 如果我有遗漏，请告诉我。
- `Thank you. I think we are clear on the next steps.` — 谢谢，我认为下一步已经明确了。
- `I will write down the decisions and action items.` — 我会记录决定和行动项。

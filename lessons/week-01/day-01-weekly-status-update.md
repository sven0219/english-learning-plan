# Day 1 — IT Status and Priorities

## 1. Daily Passage

> Good morning. Here is a quick update on our website improvement project. We have completed the first API test, and the result looks stable. The dashboard work is still in progress. The main blocker is the final review of the test data. What is the top priority for today: finishing the dashboard or repeating the API test? If we agree on the dashboard, I can complete it by EOD, or end of day. My ETA for the full test summary is tomorrow afternoon. Here, ETA means the estimated completion time. Are we aligned on this plan? I will share another update if the schedule changes, and I will confirm the next step after the review.

## 2. Key Meeting and Business Phrases

- `Here is a quick update on ...` — 下面快速汇报一下……
- `The main blocker is ...` — 当前主要阻碍是……
- `What is the top priority for today?` — 今天最优先的事项是什么？
- `Are we aligned on this plan?` — 我们是否对这个计划达成一致？
- `I will confirm the next step after ...` — 在……之后，我会确认下一步。

## 3. Abbreviations in Context

| Abbreviation | Full form | 中文含义 | Usage note |
|---|---|---|---|
| EOD | End of day | 当天结束前 | 会议、邮件和即时消息中常见；应确认具体日期和时区。 |
| ETA | Estimated time of arrival | 预计到达或完成时间 | IT会议中常指预计完成或下次更新时间，需要说明具体含义。 |

## 4. Replace and Practice

- `the first API test` → `the database test` / `the network check`
- `the dashboard` → `the monitoring page` / `the test report`
- `by EOD` → `by Friday` / `by tomorrow morning`
- `tomorrow afternoon` → `Friday morning` / `next Monday`

## 5. Questions

1. What has the team completed?
2. What is the main blocker?
3. What priority does the speaker want to confirm?
4. What do EOD and ETA mean in this passage?

## 6. Retelling Challenge

不看原文，用一分钟汇报一个虚构IT任务，并说明优先级、EOD目标和ETA。

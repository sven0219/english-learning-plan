# Day 4 — Asking for Clarification

## 1. Daily Passage

> I have a few questions about the new plan. Sorry, could you repeat the target date? I heard that the first test will start on Tuesday, but I am not sure when the final review will happen. Could you also clarify what you mean by “ready for review”? Do we need to complete all tests before the review, or can we review the first result? Let me make sure I understood you correctly. The project team will prepare the test, and I will review the result on Wednesday. After that, we will decide whether we need another test. Is that correct? Thank you for the explanation. I will update my task list and let you know if I have any other questions.

## 2. Key Meeting Phrases

- `Sorry, could you repeat ...?` — 不好意思，可以重复一下……吗？
- `Could you clarify what you mean by ...?` — 可以解释一下……是什么意思吗？
- `Let me make sure I understood you correctly.` — 请让我确认一下是否理解正确。
- `Is that correct?` — 是这样吗？
- `Thank you for the explanation.` — 谢谢你的解释。

## 3. Replace and Practice

- `the target date` → `the next step` / `the main requirement`
- `ready for review` → `ready for testing` / `complete`
- `on Wednesday` → `by Friday` / `next week`
- `another test` → `another meeting` / `more information`

## 4. Questions

1. What date does the speaker want to confirm?
2. Which phrase is unclear?
3. Who will review the result?
4. What will happen after the review?

## 5. Retelling Challenge

模拟自己在会议中没有听懂计划。使用至少三种方式请求重复、解释和确认。

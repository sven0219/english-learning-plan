# Day 4 — Access Review Clarification

## 1. Daily Passage

> FYI, or for your information, the access review is planned for Thursday. I have a few questions before we begin. Could you clarify which test accounts are included? I also heard that one request is urgent, but I am not sure what urgent means in this case. Instead of saying ASAP, or as soon as possible, could we agree on a clear deadline? ASAP can sound demanding and may mean different things to different people. Let me make sure I understood correctly. The infrastructure team will review the generic access list by Thursday morning, and the project team will confirm the result in the afternoon. Is that correct? Thank you for the explanation. I will update the plan and share a short summary after the meeting.

## 2. Key Meeting and Business Phrases

- `Could you clarify what you mean by ...?` — 可以解释一下……是什么意思吗？
- `Could we agree on a clear deadline?` — 我们可以确定一个明确的截止时间吗？
- `Let me make sure I understood you correctly.` — 请让我确认一下是否理解正确。
- `Is that correct?` — 是这样吗？
- `I will share a short summary after the meeting.` — 我会在会后分享一份简短总结。

## 3. Abbreviations in Context

| Abbreviation | Full form | 中文含义 | Usage note |
|---|---|---|---|
| FYI | For your information | 供你参考 | 常用于邮件和即时消息，通常不表示必须采取行动。 |
| ASAP | As soon as possible | 尽快 | 邮件和口语中都可见，但可能显得强硬；最好给出原因和明确期限。 |

## 4. Replace and Practice

- `the access review` → `the security review` / `the test plan review`
- `the test accounts` → `the requested permissions` / `the review items`
- `by Thursday morning` → `by EOD` / `by Friday afternoon`
- `share a short summary` → `send the final decision` / `update the task list`

## 5. Questions

1. What review is planned for Thursday?
2. What scope does the speaker want to clarify?
3. Why does the speaker prefer a clear deadline to ASAP?
4. How are FYI and ASAP normally used?

## 6. Retelling Challenge

模拟澄清一个虚构IT任务的范围和期限，并正确解释FYI和ASAP的语气。

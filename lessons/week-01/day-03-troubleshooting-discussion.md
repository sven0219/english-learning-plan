# Day 3 — Troubleshooting Discussion

## 1. Daily Passage

> I would like to share the latest status of the issue. During our test, we noticed that the application was slower than expected. We first checked the recent change, but it looked normal. We then repeated the test and saw the same problem. Could you clarify whether the problem happens every time or only during busy periods? If I understand correctly, the support team will collect more information this afternoon. One possible option is to compare the new result with an earlier test. I will review the information and prepare a short summary. We do not have the exact cause yet, but the main function is still available. I will get back to you when we know more.

## 2. Key Meeting Phrases

- `I would like to share the latest status of the issue.` — 我想介绍一下问题的最新状态。
- `Could you clarify whether ...?` — 可以说明一下是否……吗？
- `One possible option is ...` — 一个可行的方案是……
- `We do not have the exact cause yet.` — 我们还没有找到准确原因。
- `I will get back to you when we know more.` — 有更多信息后我会回复你。

## 3. Replace and Practice

- `slower than expected` → `unavailable for a short time` / `showing an error`
- `during busy periods` → `after the change` / `in the morning`
- `collect more information` → `repeat the test` / `review the settings`
- `this afternoon` → `tomorrow morning`

## 4. Questions

1. What problem did the team notice?
2. What did they check first?
3. What clarification does the speaker request?
4. Does the team know the exact cause?

## 5. Retelling Challenge

描述一个完全虚构的问题：现象、已经检查的内容、需要澄清的问题和下一步。

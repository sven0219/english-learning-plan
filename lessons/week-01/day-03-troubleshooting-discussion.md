# Day 3 — Service Issue and SLA

## 1. Daily Passage

> During today’s service review, we discussed a slow response from an online application. The monitoring system shows that requests are taking longer than usual, but the service is still available. Our SLA, or service level agreement, requires us to respond to important issues within an agreed time. The support lead will be the POC, or point of contact, for this investigation. Could you walk us through what has already been checked? So far, the team has reviewed the network and database, but the cause is not clear. One possible option is to compare today’s result with an earlier test. Let us take the detailed analysis offline and follow up in two hours. Please keep me posted on any major change.

## 2. Key Meeting and Business Phrases

- `Could you walk us through ...?` — 可以带我们了解一下……吗？
- `One possible option is ...` — 一个可行的方案是……
- `Let us take this offline.` — 这个问题我们会后单独讨论。
- `Please keep me posted.` — 请随时向我同步进展。
- `The cause is not clear yet.` — 原因目前还不明确。

## 3. Abbreviations in Context

| Abbreviation | Full form | 中文含义 | Usage note |
|---|---|---|---|
| SLA | Service level agreement | 服务级别协议 | 常用于服务目标和责任讨论，口语和文档中都常见。 |
| POC | Point of contact | 联系人 | 本文指负责人；在其他技术语境中也可能表示proof of concept（概念验证）。 |

## 4. Replace and Practice

- `a slow response` → `a short interruption` / `a login error`
- `the network and database` → `the application and cache` / `the recent change`
- `the support lead` → `the project lead` / `the service owner`
- `in two hours` → `by EOD` / `tomorrow morning`

## 5. Questions

1. What problem is the team discussing?
2. What has already been checked?
3. Who is the POC for the investigation?
4. What does the SLA require in this fictional scenario?

## 6. Retelling Challenge

描述一个完全虚构的服务问题，说明SLA、POC、已检查内容和下一次更新时间。

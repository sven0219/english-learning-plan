# Day 10 — Change Risk and Action Items

## 1. Daily Passage

> Before we approve a small configuration change, I would like to review the risks and action items. The test result is positive, but the rollback check is still WIP, or work in progress. One person suggested making the change tomorrow. I understand the reason, but I have one concern: without a completed rollback check, recovery may take longer if a problem occurs. From a business perspective, waiting one day creates less risk than an avoidable interruption. The final change time is TBD, or to be determined, after the check. Are we aligned on this approach? Let me summarize what we agreed. I will complete the rollback check, the project team will review the result, and the service owner will confirm the time. Please let me know if I missed anything.

## 2. Key Meeting and Business Phrases

- `I would like to review the risks and action items.` — 我想回顾一下风险和行动项。（适合会议开场，语气正式而中性。）
- `I understand the reason, but I have one concern.` — 我理解这个理由，但我有一个担忧。（用于会议中礼貌表达不同意见。）
- `From a business perspective, ...` — 从业务角度看……（适合会议和邮件中说明建议的业务理由。）
- `Let me summarize what we agreed.` — 我来总结一下我们达成的共识。（主要用于会议结尾。）
- `Please let me know if I missed anything.` — 如果我有遗漏，请告诉我。（适合会议、邮件和即时消息，语气协作。）

## 3. Abbreviations in Context

| Abbreviation | Full form | 中文含义 | Usage note |
|---|---|---|---|
| WIP | Work in progress | 进行中的工作 | 常见于任务看板、状态列表和即时消息；本课表示检查尚未完成。 |
| TBD | To be determined | 待决定 | 常见于计划和状态表；表示尚未作出决定，与已有初步安排但待确认的TBC不同。 |

## 4. Replace and Practice

- `a small configuration change` → `a database update` / `an application release`
- `the rollback check` → `the security review` / `the final performance test`
- `waiting one day` → `reducing the scope` / `adding one more test`
- `confirm the time` → `approve the plan` / `send the final decision`

## 5. Questions

1. Which task is still WIP?
2. What concern does the speaker raise?
3. Why does the speaker recommend waiting one day?
4. What are the three action items at the end?

## 6. Retelling Challenge

不看原文，讨论一个虚构变更的风险，礼貌表达担忧，并总结WIP事项、TBD决定、负责人和下一步。

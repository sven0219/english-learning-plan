# Day 8 — Monitoring Review and Follow-Up

## 1. Daily Passage

> During today’s monitoring review, we discussed repeated warnings from the application. The service is available, but the response time is sometimes slower than usual. The support team has checked the network and database, and no clear cause has been found. For now, the team can continue BAU, or business as usual, while one person completes a deeper check. However, we should not ignore the risk. Our SLA, or service level agreement, requires an update within the agreed time. Could you walk us through the latest result? One possible option is to compare the warnings with the last performance test. Let us take the detailed analysis offline. The support lead will own the investigation and send an update in two hours. Please keep us posted if the service condition changes.

## 2. Key Meeting and Business Phrases

- `For now, the team can continue ...` — 目前，团队可以继续……（会议和状态更新中用于说明临时安排。）
- `We should not ignore the risk.` — 我们不应忽视这个风险。（适合会议中提醒风险，语气直接但中性。）
- `Could you walk us through the latest result?` — 可以带我们了解一下最新结果吗？（主要用于会议口语，请对方逐步说明。）
- `One possible option is to ...` — 一个可行方案是……（用于会议中提出建议，语气温和。）
- `Please keep us posted if ...` — 如果……，请随时向我们同步。（适合会议、邮件和即时消息。）

## 3. Abbreviations in Context

| Abbreviation | Full form | 中文含义 | Usage note |
|---|---|---|---|
| BAU | Business as usual | 日常业务或正常运营 | 常用于会议、邮件和状态报告；表示继续常规工作，而不是停止服务。 |
| SLA | Service level agreement | 服务级别协议 | 常用于服务目标、响应时间和责任讨论，口语与文档中都常见。 |

## 4. Replace and Practice

- `repeated warnings` → `a login error` / `a short interruption`
- `the network and database` → `the application and cache` / `the recent change`
- `compare the warnings` → `review the logs` / `repeat the test`
- `in two hours` → `by EOD` / `tomorrow morning`

## 5. Questions

1. What has the monitoring system shown?
2. What has the support team already checked?
3. Why can the team continue BAU for now?
4. Who owns the investigation, and when is the next update?

## 6. Retelling Challenge

描述一个完全虚构的监控问题，说明服务状态、已检查内容、BAU安排、SLA要求、负责人和下次更新时间。

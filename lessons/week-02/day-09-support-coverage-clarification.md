# Day 9 — Support Coverage Clarification

## 1. Daily Passage

> At today’s planning meeting, we discussed support for an access test next week. One team member will be OOO, or out of office, on Monday. The calendar also shows PTO, or paid time off, for another team member on Tuesday. Could you clarify who can review the test result on those days? I do not want to assume that an OOO message means the same thing as approved PTO. Let me make sure I understood correctly: the infrastructure team will run the test, and the project lead will review the result on Wednesday. If an urgent question comes up before then, the support lead will be the point of contact. Does that work for everyone? I will write down the ownership and dates, then share a short summary after the meeting.

## 2. Key Meeting and Business Phrases

- `Could you clarify who can ...?` — 可以说明一下谁能够……吗？（主要用于会议和邮件中澄清负责人。）
- `I do not want to assume that ...` — 我不想想当然地认为……（适合会议中礼貌检查假设。）
- `Let me make sure I understood correctly.` — 请让我确认一下是否理解正确。（主要用于会议口语复述信息。）
- `Does that work for everyone?` — 这个安排对大家都合适吗？（用于会议中确认可行性，语气友好。）
- `I will write down the ownership and dates.` — 我会记录负责人和日期。（用于会议结尾确认行动项。）

## 3. Abbreviations in Context

| Abbreviation | Full form | 中文含义 | Usage note |
|---|---|---|---|
| OOO | Out of office | 不在办公室 | 常见于日历、邮件自动回复和即时消息状态；只说明暂时不在，不一定表示休假。 |
| PTO | Paid time off | 带薪休假 | 常用于休假安排、日历和内部沟通；不同地区的制度可能不同，使用时不必询问私人原因。 |

## 4. Replace and Practice

- `an access test` → `a performance test` / `a release review`
- `on Monday` → `on Thursday morning` / `next Friday`
- `the infrastructure team` → `the testing team` / `the support team`
- `review the result on Wednesday` → `send feedback by EOD` / `confirm the result tomorrow`

## 5. Questions

1. What activity needs support next week?
2. Why does the speaker ask about review coverage?
3. What is the difference between OOO and PTO in this passage?
4. Who will answer an urgent question before Wednesday?

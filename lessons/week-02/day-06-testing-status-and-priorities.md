# Day 6 — Testing Status and Priorities

## 1. Daily Passage

> Today, I would like to give a quick update on the application test. The login test is complete, and the result looks stable. The reporting page is WIP, or work in progress, because we are waiting for sample data. The main blocker may affect Friday’s review. What is the top priority: completing the reporting page or checking the login result again? From a business perspective, finishing the page first will give the project team a complete test flow. If we agree, I can finish the first version by EOD, or end of day. The next step is to verify the page tomorrow morning. Are we aligned on this plan? I will keep the team posted and share another update if the schedule changes.

## 2. Key Meeting and Business Phrases

- `I would like to give a quick update on ...` — 我想快速汇报一下……（主要用于会议口语，语气中性。）
- `What is the top priority: A or B?` — A和B之中，哪一个最优先？（适合会议和即时消息，可帮助团队明确取舍。）
- `From a business perspective, ...` — 从业务角度看……（适合会议和邮件，先说明判断角度。）
- `Are we aligned on this plan?` — 我们是否对这个计划达成一致？（主要用于会议确认共识。）
- `I will keep the team posted.` — 我会持续向团队同步进展。（常用于会议、邮件和即时消息。）

## 3. Abbreviations in Context

| Abbreviation | Full form | 中文含义 | Usage note |
|---|---|---|---|
| WIP | Work in progress | 进行中的工作 | 常见于状态列表、任务看板和即时消息；表示尚未完成，不一定表示存在问题。 |
| EOD | End of day | 当天结束前 | 会议、邮件和即时消息中常见；应确认具体日期和时区，避免期限不清。 |

## 4. Replace and Practice

- `the login test` → `the database check` / `the API test`
- `the reporting page` → `the monitoring page` / `the test summary`
- `waiting for sample data` → `waiting for one review` / `checking a configuration`
- `by EOD` → `by tomorrow morning` / `by Friday afternoon`

## 5. Questions

1. Which test is complete?
2. Why is the reporting page still WIP?
3. Which task does the speaker recommend doing first?
4. What do WIP and EOD mean in this passage?

## 6. Retelling Challenge

不看原文，用一分钟汇报一个虚构测试任务，说明已完成内容、WIP事项、主要阻碍、优先级和EOD目标。
